Lay's stacked its potato crisps into a tall, resealable canister — uniform, stackable, lunchbox-proof. The Original crisps left shelves as the concept evolved into Lay's Stax. Sealed canisters from the original run are what trade now.

Why it’s rare

Discontinued; the stacked-crisp canister concept carried on under the Lay's Stax name.

Discontinued, but still circulating secondhand — traded as collectibles, labeled as such.

Policies

Shipping, returns & refunds, privacy, and terms — in plain words, because a store that takes payment owes you the fine print up front.

Last updated Oct 16, 2026. Jump to: Shipping · Returns & Refunds · Privacy · Terms

Shipping

YumRare is supplier-routed: the source seller ships your item directly to you. Shipping cost is the supplier’s, shown before you pay — passed through transparently, never padded in silence. Expected shipping windows, including longer windows for international sources, are stated on the item and at checkout before payment. Some items carry heat and shelf-life notes (chocolate in summer, carbonation in glass); when a source can’t ship an item safely, we won’t list it. Orders route to the source within one business day, and supplier tracking is relayed to you whenever it exists. At launch, YumRare serves US customers; sources may be international.

Returns & Refunds

Food is final-sale except when an item arrives damaged or is simply the wrong item — then we make it right. If a source fails after you order (sold out, price changed beyond its band, can’t ship), your authorization is voided or your payment refunded in full, automatically. We never substitute a different item silently — ever. Physical returns, when a return is appropriate, ship to the source supplier under its policy; YumRare coordinates the return and issues your refund on the supplier’s confirmation. Your card is authorized at checkout and captured only when the source confirms your item is secured, so a failed source never leaves you charged for a ghost.

Privacy

We collect only what an order needs: the details our payment processors require to take payment, and the shipping details a source needs to deliver. If you join the Rare Finds list, your email is used for rare-finds alerts and nothing else — never sold, never rented. We do not sell personal data in any form. Demand reporting, if it ever exists, is aggregate and anonymized only. Questions about your data go through the store contact channel on the About page and are answered within one business day.

Terms

YumRare is the seller of record for routed orders: we take your payment and own refunds, customer service, and the promises on this page. Items labeled “Collectible — not for consumption” are sold as collectibles only and are not marketed as food to eat. Brand and product names are used nominatively — to identify the genuine item — and belong to their makers. Prices on routed items are set by YumRare and stated before payment; marketplace prices are set by their sellers. Allergen and ingredient information always comes from the manufacturer’s own listing. These policies may be updated; the version on this page at the time of your order is the one that applies to it.

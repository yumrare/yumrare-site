About YumRare

YumRare is the rare-snack store: the snacks your grocery store doesn’t carry, found and gathered in one small, honest shop.

The promise

We find it. Sellers ship it. YumRare holds no inventory. When you order, we route your purchase to a vetted source seller who ships it to you — and we stay responsible for your experience the whole way. Every rarity claim on this site is a fact with its reason attached: sold only in that country, made for that market, back for this run. If we can’t verify it, we don’t badge it.

Contact

Questions about an order, a business inquiry, or a snack you think belongs on these shelves? The Contact Us page carries the store’s form — messages go straight to the store’s inbox and are answered within one business day.

Questions, answered

How long will my order take to arrive?

It depends on the source. Every item states its expected shipping window — including longer windows for international sources — before you pay, and YumRare launches serving US customers. Orders route to the source within one business day, and supplier tracking is relayed to you whenever it exists.

What happens if a seller can’t fulfill my order?

Your refund is automatic. Your card is authorized at checkout and captured only when a source confirms your item is secured, so if a source falls through — sold out, or unable to ship — the authorization is voided or your payment refunded in full. We never substitute a different item silently.

Can I return a snack I changed my mind about?

Food is final-sale, except when an item arrives damaged or is simply the wrong item — then we make it right. When a return is appropriate, it ships to the source supplier under its policy; YumRare coordinates the return and issues your refund on the supplier’s confirmation.

Why does checkout say ’opens at launch’?

This storefront is a staging preview. YumRare’s own checkout switches on when payment processing is set up in the store’s name; until then, the Buy button stays visibly labeled, and every snack’s page links out to the maker or retailer that stocks it.

Suggest a snack

Know a snack that is still made somewhere but never reaches your grocery store? That is exactly what this shelf hunts. Send it through the Contact page — pick “Suggest a snack,” tell us what it is and where it is sold, and it goes into the verification queue. If it checks out as current, genuinely hard to find here, and made by a major snack corporation, it can earn a slot in the next rotation.

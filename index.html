How YumRare works

Three steps, no warehouse, no surprises — the promise printed on the tin.

1We find it.
Hunted down and verified by hand — every find carries its rarity badge with the reason attached.
2You check out here.
One payment on YumRare, with several ways to pay — and your card is captured only once a source confirms your item.
3Sellers ship it.
Your order routes to a vetted source seller who ships it to you, tracking relayed, windows stated before you pay.

Rarity, in plain language

Every badge on every card comes with its reason. If we can’t say why it’s rare, it doesn’t get the badge.

Import Only

Flavors and products sold in other countries — reachable here through importers and marketplaces.

Limited Run

Limited editions, seasonal drops, and comeback editions that arrive in waves.

Regional Exclusive

Sold in one region, country, or state only — never national.

Small Batch

Indie and small-maker snacks with thin distribution and short runs.

Gone But Trading

Discontinued, but still circulating secondhand — traded as collectibles, labeled as such.

Stocking an office, a party, or a resale shelf?

Case variants — cases of 12 or 24 — ride the same routing as single items, just with bigger baskets. Case options appear on item cards wherever a source sells by the case. No trade accounts, no separate wholesale operation: a case is simply a bigger find.

A taste of home, shipped

Living far from the snacks you grew up with? The Import Only shelf is built for care packages and homesick pantries — and every bundle on YumRare lists its contents up front. You always know exactly what you’re buying before you pay.

Every rarity, with its reason

Badges are facts, not adjectives — sold only there, gone since then, back for this run. Checkable, every time.

No fake scarcity

No countdown timers, no “only 3 left” theater. When something sells out, the card says so — and points you to a watch instead of a dead end.

Refunds without a fight

Your card is authorized at checkout and captured only when a source confirms your item. If the source falls through, the refund is automatic.
